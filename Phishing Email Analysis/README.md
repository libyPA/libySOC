# Phishing Email Analysis & IOC Investigation

## Overview

Hands-on phishing email investigation focused on **email header analysis, authentication checks, infrastructure analysis, and IOC identification**.

The analysis was performed on a simulated phishing email in a controlled environment.

## Objectives

* Analyze email headers and sender information.
* Examine SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results.
* Investigate sender and Return-Path domains.
* Analyze URLs, domains, and IP addresses.
* Identify indicators of compromise.
* Determine whether the email showed characteristics of phishing.

## Investigation Focus

The investigation examined:

* Sender identity
* Email headers
* SPF authentication
* DKIM authentication
* DMARC authentication
* Return-Path
* Mail routing information
* URLs and domains
* Source IP indicators
* Phishing indicators

## Key Findings

The analysis identified multiple authentication and infrastructure inconsistencies that indicated the email was suspicious.

The investigation also identified relevant domains, IP addresses, and URLs as potential indicators for further investigation.
